Controls

  • Steer: ← → arrow keys, or A and D.
  • Start / drive again: Space or Return.
  • Pause: Esc. Sound on/off: M. Settings: ⌘ ,

Scoring

  • You score one point per metre driven. The game speeds up the further you go.
  • Near miss: pass a car with very little room to spare for bonus points. Do several quickly for a ×2, ×3… combo.

Swerve Pro

  • A one-time purchase through the App Store. It unlocks 3 extra cars and 6 extra paint colours. All cars drive the same.
  • New Mac: open Swerve, go to the Garage and click Restore Purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds: purchases are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Performance

  • Swerve pauses itself and uses no power when you switch to another app.
  • If the game feels slow, make the window smaller.
