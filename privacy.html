Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

The short version

Swerve: Night Drive is a driving game for the Mac. It does not collect any personal data. Nothing is uploaded, shared or tracked by the game.

What Swerve stores on your Mac

  • Settings: sound on or off, volume and camera view.
  • Your garage: the car and paint you picked.
  • Your best score.

What Swerve does not do

  • No accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • Swerve has no servers and sends nothing to us.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, your best score is sent to Apple's leaderboard so you can compare scores with other players. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can sign out of Game Center in System Settings at any time.

Purchases

Swerve Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles your payment. Swerve only learns whether Pro is unlocked; it never sees your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Deleting your data

Delete Swerve and the folder ~/Library/Containers/app.swerve.Swerve. Scores already on the Game Center leaderboard are managed by Apple.

Children

Swerve collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
